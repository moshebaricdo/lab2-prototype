**1: Create a New Feature**

Draw a card from the deck and prompt AI to create that feature. Re-prompt AI as needed.

**2: Save with a Comment**

When the feature looks good, navigate to the Version History tab. Describe your changes and click **Save version**.

**3: Revert as Needed**

After 5 minutes, or when prompted by your teacher, revert to the most recent version you saved with a description.

---

**Why go back to an old version?**

Saving versions lets you try bold changes without losing your last good state. Reverting helps you compare what changed, recover from experiments that did not work out, and practice the same version-control habits professional developers use every day.
