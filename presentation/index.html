Designing

How AI changed the way I prototype, taking me from static mockups to functional, in-context prototypes I can actually use, test, and share.

Moshe BariSr. Product Designer
The Challenge

A mockup shows the product.
It doesn’t behave like it.

Static Mockups

Can communicate appearance, but not always behavior. Hard to test and share.

→

AI-Powered Prototype

You can interact with, break it, test it, and learn from it before shipping.

More and more of the product experience lives in AI interactions, system behavior, and feedback loops. Those things are difficult to evaluate in static designs.

The Workflow

Bring the idea to life.

  • Build. Created a fully functional Lab 2 environment that I can quickly iterate on, add to, and explore in.
  • Use. As I build, I can actually use the product in realtime to test both on myself and with users.
  • Refine. As I learn, test, and collect feedback I can keep refining the product until it's ready to handoff.

Impact

What this unlocked.

  • I can test the actual experience — conversations with Tutor, validation, level flows — instead of a picture of it.
  • I hand anyone a special link and they land in the exact prototype. No backend, no setup.
  • One setup powers all of our different labs and level types in the Lab2 framework, so I can explore ideas in cohesive ways.
